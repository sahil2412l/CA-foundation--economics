# CA Foundation Business Economics & BCK Application (`eco`)

An interactive preparation platform for **CA Foundation Paper 4: Business Economics & Macroeconomics / BCK**, modeled on the modern design system and gamification architecture of the Quantitative Aptitude app.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **10 Core Chapters**: Business Economics scope, Demand & Supply, Cost Curves, Market Structures, Business Cycles, National Income, Public Finance, Monetary Policy, International Trade & BCK.
- **Sub-Exercise Unit Practice**: Individual practice units with `🎯 Start` buttons filtering the quiz to that unit.
- **Economics Calculators**: Price Elasticity of Demand (Point & Arc methods), Break-Even Point, and Investment Multiplier.
- **Flashcards Revision Engine**: Formula & concept flip cards.
- **Multi-Filter Question Bank Data Table**: Real-time filtering by keyword, chapter, sub-exercise, and difficulty with step-by-step solution expansion.
- **Gamification & Cookie Persistence**: 100 Levels with rank titles (Novice → Demand Theorist → Market Analyst → Policy Strategist → AIR 1 Economist).
- **Responsive Layout**: Sticky top navbar with streak tracker, direct email, syllabus notice, quick search window, and collapsible sidebar.
